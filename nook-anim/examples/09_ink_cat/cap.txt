Global Metadata
Instrumental Chinese ink-wash short film score, in a pentatonic mode at about 76 BPM in 4/4, quiet and poetic with a touch of playful humor. The mood moves from a still night by the river, to a playful chase, to yearning, to a small gentle disappointment, and a serene, tender ending. The sound is sparse and airy with lots of space, like a small ensemble in an empty hall: plucked guqin and pipa, a soft bamboo flute, a light wooden fish knock, sparse chimes, natural reverb.

Vocal Details
The piece is fully instrumental. The bamboo flute carries the lead melody, the guqin plays deep sparse notes, the pipa adds playful plucked runs.

Arrangement
Intro: sparse deep guqin notes with long silences and a single distant chime, like water droplets.
Chase: the pipa enters with quick playful plucked runs, the wooden fish knocks a light rhythm, the flute answers in short cheeky phrases.
Yearning: the flute holds a long rising note over quiet guqin, slow and reaching.
Ripple: a soft harp-like cascade, then a hush.
Ending: the flute plays a tender falling phrase, the guqin lands on a low open fifth, and one final chime rings and fades to silence.

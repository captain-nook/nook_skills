Global Metadata
Instrumental whimsical comedy score for a stop-motion kitchen-table cartoon, in a bright major key at about 110 BPM in 4/4. The mood moves from proud, bouncy walking, to sneaky tiptoeing mischief, to a stretch of frozen suspense, a burst of comic panic, and a cheeky, fun ending. The sound is intimate and close-miked, like a small ensemble playing on a wooden kitchen table: dry, clear highs from marimba and xylophone, plucked pizzicato strings, a light woodblock, soft rounded low end, gentle room ambience and natural dynamics.

Vocal Details
The piece is fully instrumental. The marimba carries the lead melody, the pizzicato strings play the bass, the xylophone adds quick comic accents.

Arrangement
Intro: a bouncy marimba walking theme over pizzicato bass and a light woodblock, proud and cheerful.
Sneaking: the melody drops to staccato tiptoe notes in a lower register, pizzicato strings creep upward on every beat, sparse and cheeky, with short silent gaps.
Suspense: everything stops except a single held pizzicato note and a quiet marimba tremolo, leaving space.
Munching: playful staccato xylophone chirps with a lopsided rhythm, comic and greedy.
Panic: the tempo seems to double with rapid marimba runs and xylophone glissandi, then a sudden stop.
Ending: a cheeky bouncing coda, and it lands on a final short bright chord with a woodblock tick.

Global Metadata
Instrumental score for a paper-collage short film about a rainy day that gets torn open into sunshine, in a major key, starting slow and melancholic at about 70 BPM and blooming into a joyful, uplifting 110 BPM in 4/4. The mood moves from grey loneliness, to a spark of resolve, to a big warm release, then to a light, happy floating ending. The sound is warm, intimate and tactile: soft felt piano, gentle glockenspiel, plucked ukulele, light brushed percussion, a warm cello, natural room ambience.

Vocal Details
The piece is fully instrumental. A felt piano plays a slow, sparse melody at the start, later the glockenspiel and ukulele carry a bright theme.

Arrangement
Intro: a single felt piano in a low-key, slow minor-feeling melody with long pauses, like raindrops, with a very quiet cello pad.
Resolve: a soft rising figure on the piano, the cello swells, a light brush snare starts.
Release: a bright glockenspiel sparkle and a full major chord burst open, then the ukulele strums, hand claps and shaker start a happy groove.
Joy: the glockenspiel and piano play the full happy theme, ukulele and cello support, bright and airy.
Ending: the groove thins out, and the piece settles on a warm major chord with a final glockenspiel note.
